Gamepad equivalents: the eight action keys are the D-pad and face buttons; Command, Shift, Option and Control are the skill-panel triggers; ` is Target Friendly / Target Hostile; Z is the Menu button; Shift+` is Change Focus Frame.

Before playing on a Mac, turn on Options → Mac → "Disable OS shortcuts", or Command+Q quits the game.
